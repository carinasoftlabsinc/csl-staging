6. Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

Where Carina Softlabs processes personal data on behalf of a client, we act as a Data Processor, and the client acts as the Data Controller, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

6.1 Scope of Processing

  • On documented instructions from the Data Controller
  • For the purpose of delivering agreed services
  • In compliance with applicable data protection laws

6.2 Processor Obligations

  • Maintain confidentiality of processed data
  • Implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures
  • Not engage sub-processors without appropriate safeguards
  • Assist the Controller in responding to data subject requests
  • Notify the Controller of any data breach without undue delay
  • Delete or return data upon contract termination (unless legally required to retain)

6.3 Sub-Processors

We may engage trusted third-party infrastructure providers (e.g., cloud hosting, CRM systems), subject to confidentiality and data protection commitments.

A separate DPA document can be executed upon client request.
